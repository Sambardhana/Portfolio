Research Experience 🧠

Deep Learning-Based Brain Tumor Segmentation Using MRI

Journal Manuscript — In Preparation

Working on a deep-learning-based approach for automated brain tumor segmentation using MRI scans and the BraTS2020 dataset, with the aim of supporting accurate medical image analysis.

Research Focus

  • Implementation and comparison of U-Net, Attention U-Net, ResUNet, and EfficientUNet architectures.
  • Experimentation with BCE + Dice Loss, Weighted BCE, Focal Loss, and Dice Loss.
  • Patient-level dataset splitting to prevent data leakage.
  • Evaluation using Dice Coefficient, IoU, Precision, Recall, and HD95.

Tools & Technologies

Python · TensorFlow/Keras · NumPy · Pandas · BraTS2020 · Google Colab · Jupyter Notebook

Research manuscript currently in preparation. Journal selection has not yet been confirmed.

Investigating automated identification and segmentation of brain tumor regions from MRI scans using deep-learning-based semantic segmentation approaches.

Research Objective

To study methods that can accurately segment tumor regions in brain MRI images, reducing dependence on entirely manual segmentation and supporting medical image analysis.

Architectures Studied

U-Net Attention U-Net ResUNet EfficientUNet

Loss Functions

BCE + Dice Loss Weighted BCE Focal Loss Dice Loss

Dataset & Technologies
